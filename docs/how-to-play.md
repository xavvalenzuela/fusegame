# How to Play StarFuse

## Objective
Fuse matching tiles to score as many points as possible before the 60-second timer runs out.

---

## The Basics

1. **Tap two matching tiles** on the grid to fuse them.
2. Fusing removes both tiles, scores points, and spawns a new tile.
3. Keep fusing — the clock is always ticking.

---

## Combos

Fuse tiles quickly back-to-back to build a **combo chain**. Your combo multiplier increases with each consecutive fuse. Let too much time pass between fuses and the combo resets.

Higher combo = more points per fuse.

---

## Power-Ups

Certain colored star tiles trigger power-ups when fused:

| Tile | Power-Up | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 🔵 Blue Star | Time Slow | Slows the countdown timer |
| 🔴 Red Star | Score Double | Doubles all points earned |
| 🟢 Green Star | Multiplier Boost | Adds +5 to your combo multiplier |

Activate all three at the same time to stack their effects.

---

## Pre-Game Boosts

Before starting, you can watch a short ad to begin the game with a bonus:

- **⏱ +10 Seconds** — extra time on the clock
- **🔵 Time Slow** — blue star power active from the start
- **🔴 Score Double** — red star power active from the start
- **🟢 Multiplier Boost** — green star power active from the start

Tap a boost card on the home screen, watch the ad, then tap **Start with Boost**.

---

## Continue

When the timer hits zero, you have 5 seconds to watch an ad and get **30 extra seconds** to keep playing. Your score carries over.

---

## Leaderboard

Your score is automatically submitted after each game. Tap **🏆 Leaderboard** to see the global rankings. Set your display name before playing to appear on the board.

---

## Tips

- Keep fusing without gaps to maximize your combo multiplier.
- Save your ad continues for your best runs.
- Stacking all three power-ups at once is the fastest way to a high score.
- The timer ticks louder in the final 10 seconds — that's your cue to speed up.
