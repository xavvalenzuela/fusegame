# StarFuse — Closed Testing Instructions

Thanks for helping test StarFuse! This guide will get you set up and walk you through what to try.

---

## Step 1 — Accept the Testing Invite

1. Open the testing invite link sent to your email (must be a Google account).
2. Tap **Become a tester**.
3. Tap the **download it on Google Play** link — this takes you to the Play Store listing.
4. Install the app normally.

> If you don't see the install button right away, wait 1–2 minutes after accepting and refresh the Play Store page.

---

## Step 2 — First Launch

- Open **StarFuse**.
- You'll be asked to enter a **display name** — this is what shows on the leaderboard. You can change it later in the pause menu.
- The home screen shows your best score and a leaderboard button.

---

## What to Test

Work through these in order if possible, but any feedback helps.

### Core Game Loop
1. Tap **START** on the home screen.
2. The game gives you **60 seconds**. Tap two matching tiles to fuse them.
3. Fusing tiles scores points and spawns new tiles. Keep chaining fusions.
4. Try to build a **combo** — consecutive fusions without pausing increase your multiplier.
5. Watch the timer run out and see your final score.

### Power-Ups (in-game)
Fusing certain colored star tiles activates power-ups:
- **Blue star** → Time Slow (slows the countdown)
- **Red star** → Score Double (2× points)
- **Green star** → Multiplier Boost (adds +5 to your combo multiplier)

Activate all three at once for a full-screen glow effect.

### Pre-Game Boost (Watch Ad)
Before starting, you can watch a short ad to unlock a one-game bonus:
- ⏱ **+10s** — extra time
- 🔵 **Time Slow** — starts the blue star power immediately
- 🔴 **Score Double** — starts the red star power immediately
- 🟢 **Multiplier Boost** — starts the green star power immediately

Tap a boost card → watch ad → tap **START WITH BOOST**.

### Continue After Game Over
When the game ends, you have **5 seconds** to tap **Watch Ad to Continue**. This gives you 30 extra seconds to keep playing. Try it at least once.

### Leaderboard
- Tap the **🏆 Leaderboard** button on the home screen or game over screen.
- Your score is submitted automatically after each game.
- Check that your name and score appear correctly.

### Pause Menu
- During a game, tap the **⏸ pause** button (top right).
- Try resuming, changing your display name, and viewing the leaderboard from the pause menu.

### Settings
- In the pause menu, toggle **sound** on and off. Confirm audio responds.

---

## What to Look For

Please note anything that seems off, including:

- Crashes or force closes
- Tiles that don't respond to taps
- Score not updating correctly
- Timer behaving strangely (jumping, freezing)
- Leaderboard not showing your score
- Ad not loading or causing a crash
- Text that's hard to read or UI elements that feel off
- Anything that just felt confusing or frustrating
